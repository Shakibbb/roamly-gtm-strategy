- **Competitor’s name:** Airbnb Group/ viatour
- **Their core offering:** Marketplace for tours, activities, and local experiences in thousands of destinations; group booking infrastructure; host network
- **How they position themselves:** Activity Marketplace
- **Their primary audience:** Solo travellers, couples, casual groups; middle-to-upper-middle market

If they solve the coordination tax first (split payments + itinerary sync + group invites), they win the entire TAM
