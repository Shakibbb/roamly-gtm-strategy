# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FOR:** Millennial friend groups, Extended families. 4-6 friends or family 'gang' 15+. Trying some Tour Groups.
- **WHO WANT / NEED:** Spend more time together instead of rushing between platforms and negotiations.
- **WE OFFER:** One place that gives you more time to enjoy the trip.
- **BECAUSE OF:** to be able to organise the schedule payment and split everything in one go
- **DESPITE:** We don't have great coverage atm
- **BETTER THAN:** Less hassle for one friend or person to organise everything and solve many questions others would ask.
